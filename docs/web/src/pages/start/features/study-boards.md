# Study boards

Give your studies their own space with a separate study board window.

Instead of stacking studies on the chart, move them into a dedicated board. This is especially useful with a multi-monitor setup.

![Creating a new study board](/images/study-board-new.jpg)

Create a study board, then link it to a chart in your workspace. The board's studies will stay synchronized with that chart.

You can also link:
- **Crosshair** — Follow the chart cursor
- **Range** — Match scrolling and zooming

![A study board with stacked studies](/images/study-board.jpg)

Study boards keep your chart space clear while giving studies a full window. Stack as many studies as you need without crowding the price action.

For more, see [Panes and coordinates](https://plaincharts.github.io/kapelka/docs/panes-and-coordinates/).
