# Trade Desk

Open the **Trade Desk** from the **Workspace manager**. It brings your account activity together in one panel across five tabs.

![The Trade Desk, Positions tab](/images/trade-desk.jpg)

- **Console** — Communication between the app and broker, addon messages, and signals about what happens behind each action.
- **Orders** — The order book.
- **History** — Closed position history.
- **Positions** — Currently open positions.
- **Accounts** — Connected accounts.

The **Orders** tab provides a full order book. Filter it by order state, account, and time.

![The Orders tab order book](/images/orders.jpg)

Each tab is customizable. Click the gear in the top-right corner to open its in-tab settings.

The **History** tab can display a stats bar. Open **Configure** and choose which statistics to show, including:

- Net Profit
- Trades
- Hit Rate
- Profit Factor
- Balance

![Configuring the History stats bar](/images/history-stats.jpg)

Common statistics are included by default. Add your own through the provided infrastructure.
